Shatterforge · phase 1

    
A 12-storey office tower at real scale

    
Every wall, slab, column and stair is a real-size brick: storeys are 3.75 m, doors 0.875 × 2.125 m, corridors 1.75 m, steps 0.25 m. Walk in through the lobby, take the stair in the core, and knock holes in it. What falls off is simulated on the GPU as rigid pieces.
